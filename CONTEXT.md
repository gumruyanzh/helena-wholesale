# Helena Wholesale Distributions Inc

The business the website represents: a general-merchandise wholesaler and merchandise broker based at 1001 South Main Street, Suite 49, Kalispell, Montana 59901 (406-439-9079).

## Language

**Helena Wholesale**:
Short name for Helena Wholesale Distributions Inc.
_Avoid_: HWD, Helena Distributing

**General Merchandise**:
The deliberately broad description of what Helena Wholesale handles. No specific product categories are named publicly.
_Avoid_: listing specific lines (grocery, hardware, etc.)

**Wholesaler**:
Helena Wholesale acting as a seller of goods in volume to businesses.

**Merchandise Broker**:
Helena Wholesale acting as the go-between that connects sellers and buyers of goods, without necessarily taking ownership.

**Supplier**:
A manufacturer, brand or vendor that wants its goods sold through Helena Wholesale.
_Avoid_: vendor partner, principal

**Buyer**:
A business (retailer, reseller, institution) that purchases goods from or through Helena Wholesale. Never an individual consumer.
_Avoid_: customer, client (ambiguous across the two sides)

**Inquiry**:
A first contact from a Supplier or a Buyer asking to start working with Helena Wholesale.

**Manifest**:
The site's inquiry form, presented as a freight bill of lading. Submitting a Manifest is how a Supplier or Buyer sends an Inquiry. Each Manifest carries a reference number.
_Avoid_: contact form, quote request

## Relationships

- Helena Wholesale sits between **Suppliers** and **Buyers**; the site addresses each as a separate audience
- Every **Inquiry** comes from exactly one side: Supplier or Buyer
