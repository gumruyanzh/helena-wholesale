What we do

Two ways goods move through Helena.

We work on both sides of the trade. Sometimes we buy and resell. Sometimes we broker the deal between the maker and the shelf. Either way, you deal with one company and one point of contact.

Line A

Wholesale distribution

We buy general merchandise in volume and supply it to businesses, whether that's one mixed lot or repeat volume orders. You get a single source and a single invoice instead of chasing many vendors.

  • Volume and mixed-lot orders
  • One account, one invoice
  • Business buyers only

Line B

Merchandise brokerage

We represent suppliers' goods to business buyers and handle the introduction, the negotiation and the follow-through, so suppliers can reach new buyers without building a sales force of their own.

  • Supplier representation
  • Buyer introductions and placement
  • Negotiation on your behalf

Suppliers & Buyers

Which side of the dock are you on?

Put your goods in front of business buyers.

Manufacturers, brands and vendors work with Helena when they want more buyers without hiring a sales team. Tell us what you make and we'll tell you honestly whether we can place it.

File as a Supplier
  1. Representation. We present your goods to business buyers as your broker.
  2. Placement. We work to get your goods stocked, not just looked at.
  3. Volume outlet. We can buy in volume as a wholesaler when it suits both sides.
  4. One contact. One person handles your account from first call to delivery.

One source for the goods you need.

Retailers, resellers and institutions come to Helena to source general merchandise without managing a long list of vendors. Tell us what you're looking for and we'll get back to you with what we can do.

File as a Buyer
  1. Sourcing. Tell us what you need and we'll work to source it.
  2. Flexible lots. Mixed lots or volume orders, sized to your business.
  3. Fewer vendors. Many lines through one account.
  4. One contact. A real person you can reach, based in Kalispell.

How it works

From first word to moving goods.

  1. 01

    File a Manifest

    Tell us who you are, which side you're on and what goods are involved. It takes about two minutes.

  2. 02

    We review & reply

    Someone from Helena reads your Manifest and replies within one business day, by phone or email as you prefer, to talk it through.

  3. 03

    Goods move

    Once terms are agreed, we coordinate getting the goods from supplier to buyer.
